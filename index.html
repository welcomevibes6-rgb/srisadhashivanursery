About Us

About Sri Sadha Shiva Nursery

Sri Sadha Shiva Nursery brings over 20 years of experience from Kadiyam, Andhra Pradesh, one of India's well-known nursery-growing regions. We offer a wide range of plants for homes, gardens, landscapes, commercial spaces, and large-scale projects.

From ornamental and flowering plants to avenue, palm, indoor, outdoor, and other varieties, we provide plants to suit different landscaping and plantation requirements. Along with plant supply, we also undertake professional landscaping services and support bulk requirements and plant exports.

With quality plants, reliable supply, and professional service, we aim to bring the right greenery to every space.

“Growing greener spaces, one plant at a time.”
